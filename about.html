The Story Behind MTD

About

MTD Studio is an independent multidisciplinary creative and technical studio built around a simple idea: keep learning, keep making, and keep improving. The studio brings together architectural technology, technical drafting, 3D modelling, graphic design and creative digital experimentation. Based in Limpopo, South Africa, MTD Studio focuses on creating practical, accessible digital solutions while continuously developing its technical and creative capabilities.
