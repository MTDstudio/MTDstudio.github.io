Before you view our pricing

Every project is different. Published prices are starting prices intended to provide an indication of cost, not a fixed quotation.

Final pricing is determined after reviewing the project scope, supplied information, complexity, number of deliverables, revisions and required turnaround time. Additional work outside the agreed scope will be quoted separately or charged at the applicable hourly rate.

MTD Studio provides technical drafting, CAD conversion, documentation and 3D modelling services. These services do not constitute architectural professional services or professional sign-off. Where professional approval or statutory submission is required, the client remains responsible for appointing the appropriate registered professional.
